## Qualities

The Kin are somewhat harmonoid, though their four arms and unnerving,
backward-looking hands can be a start, initially. Their skin is smooth and
glass-like, with intricate patterns woven throughout, typically of a lighter
color, though not always. Every part of their bodies seems adapted to the sands,
every crevice of their body flush and smooth.

They have eyes, though I don't know what they look like, as they always wear
masks, seemingly from birth. I say seemingly, as I don't actually know how the
Kin reproduce. They don't have any perceptible sense of sex nor gender, a
homogenous people who seem inclined to collaborate.

## Society

An egalitarian society, largely, the Kin tend toward "familial" units, though
much larger than you or I might be used to. Everyone contributes, and at a
glance, one ignorant to their ways might presume them to be a sort of hive mind,
an automatic force that functions like ants or the like.

This is not true - there is a shocking, and assertive, sense of individuality
among them, each with their own passions, often music and art. With this in
mind, if there is any form of central leadership or beaurocracy, religious or
otherwise, it is not made known to outsiders.

They organize themselves into various groups, whose names I do not know, neither
proper names nor the word they use to describe these groups. They could perhaps
be described as tribes, although what we consider tribal may or may not depict
their social structures. Nonetheless, they are often (always?) nomadic, and
culture varies from group to group.

From observing their art and listening to their songs, however, some reverence
is clear for a variety of higher powers - sometimes it seems like they vary from
group to group, but it is not altogether clear.

But when they face danger, no people in the cosmos fights with more effortless,
seamless communication.

## History and Culture

Their creation myths are variations on a theme - they consider themselves
"children of salt", grown from the same mechanisms that formed the rocks and
structures that eventually get ground into endless sands. As mentioned
[above](#qualities), I don't know how they reproduce, so ratifying this fact
into something more concrete is difficult, but they clearly do have some
[lithoteric](../reference/reference.md/#lithoteric) elements in and on their
bodies.

Their religion is interesting - they recognize the power and importance, with
varying levels of respect and disdain, of all of the
[Elders](../elders/elders.md), although this varies between groups and across
landscapes. Seemingly universally, they worship [Chama](../elders/chama.md),
perhaps unsurprisingly, as she is seen to provide water to those who reside
here. Nevertheless, there seems to be no meaningful reference to the
[Primordia](../primordia/primordia.md) that I have encountered thus far.

Much of their storytelling, at least in the form I received, revolves around
myths about great individuals communing with or conquering grand, unknown
forces. Maybe this is where the Primordia show their faces; then again, perhaps
not.
